# Safety

This repo is a public-figure persona-steering experiment. The outputs are
synthetic model generations, not statements by or about the real people.

- Do not publish raw “I am …” generations as real quotes.
- Do not use this to impersonate a real person for deception, harassment, or
  financial gain.
- The directions cause factual contamination: identity claims leak into
  unrelated questions. Do not deploy them.
- The experiments use benign prompts and no political figures. Keep it that way.
- The single-neuron and trio negative results are as important as the positives.
